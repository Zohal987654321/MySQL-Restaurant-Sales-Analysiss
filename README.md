# MySQL-Restaurant-Sales-Analysis
This project dives into restaurant sales and customer behavior using SQL, uncovering key insights to optimize operations and drive revenue growth.
Key Findings & Business Applications:
✅ Menu Optimization – Identified bestsellers and underperforming dishes to refine offerings.
 ✅ Revenue Growth – Analyzed high-spend orders to improve pricing & promotions.
 ✅ Inventory Management – Forecasted ingredient demand based on order trends.
 ✅ Customer Insights – Examined purchasing behavior to enhance marketing & loyalty programs.
 ✅ Operational Efficiency – Streamlined processes by understanding high-volume orders.
This project highlights the power of SQL & data analysis in transforming restaurant operations. Looking forward to applying these insights in real-world business scenarios! 💡📈
